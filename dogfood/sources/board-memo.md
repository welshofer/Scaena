# Northwind Analytics: notes for the Q3 FY26 board review

## Pipeline
Qualified opportunities converted at 31% this quarter, our best rate in two years. Inbound leads fell 18% after we paused paid search in July, so fewer opportunities reached qualification.

## Small customers
SMB logo churn reached 4.9%. Exit interviews name price over value most often. One agency owner (12 people) said in August 2026: "We love the dashboards, but at our size the price went up faster than what we get from it."

## Q4 scenarios
- Base: $52.0M ARR if enterprise closes at Q3 rates.
- Upside: $53.4M ARR if both Fortune 500 pilots convert.
- Downside: $50.6M ARR if SMB churn reaches 6%.

## A cheaper SMB tier
Modelling suggests it cuts SMB churn by about 40%, simplifies packaging, and wins back price-sensitive prospects. It puts $1.2M of ARR at risk from downgrades, needs sales retraining in Q4, and dilutes gross margin by about 0.4 points.
